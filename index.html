🚫 The Payola Firewall

Published day one. Non-negotiable. Changed only by Black.

  • The Booth sells written feedback ONLY. One credit buys one written verdict on one track.
  • Placements are never sold and never influenced by payment. No playlist add, curator pitch, sync placement, or editorial favor can be bought — not on any platform, not ever.
  • A good verdict earns the artist nothing but the truth. A bad verdict costs $1 and saves months of pitching the wrong lane.
  • Reviewers caught trading band upgrades, playlist adds, or "guaranteed" results for side payment are removed permanently, and a payola_adjacency deal-breaker flag fires on every verdict they touched.
