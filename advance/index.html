2 · Methodology PUBLISHED

Method id: royalty-advance-empirical-80/v1 · nominal coverage 80%.

  1. Trend fit. Ordinary least-squares trend on the trailing monthly earnings series (minimum 3 months; 12 recommended). All inputs must be non-negative numbers.
  2. Forward prediction. Project the trend 12 months ahead (each month floored at $0), sum the predictions.
  3. Advance point. advance_ratio × predicted next-12-month total. Default ratio 0.6 (editable above) — the Amuse Fast Forward shape: an advance against a share of predicted earnings.
  4. Error bars. The published half-width ±20.56% is the empirical 80th percentile of |relative residual| from 9 walk-forward back-test windows on a 12-month series (see §3). Output range = point × (1 ± 0.2056). Nominal coverage 80% — the back-test measured 8/9 windows inside.
  5. Stamping. Every estimate carries the method id, the input SHA-256, and the q used — reproducible from the inputs above. The headline back-test claim is independently stamped with the error-bar tool (Wilson interval, provenance pass, confidence tier weak — reported as-is): backtest-stamped.json.

Streams shortcut: monthly earnings = (streams × per-stream $) ÷ 12, i.e. a flat monthly shape assumption — disclosed, not data. The intelligence store's stream time-series exists but distributor royalty statements are not yet wired in, so v1 ingests pasted history.
